How EUMO Works

Built to help you understand your emotional patterns, gently and consistently.

EUMO is a reflection system, not a diagnosis platform. It combines daily check-ins, guided prompts, journaling, and adaptive insights so you can notice what affects your mood over time.

Start Reflection Read Consent

Step 1

Check In

Log one mood per day. This becomes your base emotional signal for trend tracking.

Step 2

Reflect

Answer prompts and journal freely. EUMO stores your entries and context with your account.

Step 3

Understand

See emotional stability trends, weekly progress, and contextual suggestions from your own activity.

Step 4

Grow

Build consistency with micro-goals, reflection streaks, and adaptive prompts over time.

What EUMO Includes

  • Daily mood check-ins and emotional weather.
  • Know Yourself assessment and trait-based summaries.
  • Private journaling with optional AI-sharing consent per note.
  • Progress visuals, timeline insights, and adaptive suggestions.

What EUMO Is Not

  • Not a replacement for therapy or emergency support.
  • Not a clinical diagnostic tool.
  • Not a one-time test; it improves with regular use.
  • Not designed to pressure you into perfect streaks.

Privacy

You control AI visibility

Journal entries are private by default unless you explicitly allow AI sharing.

Consistency

Inputs shape your score

Emotional stability is calculated from your real activity, not random placeholders.

Safety

Support is always available

Support page includes helplines and pathways to professional help resources.
